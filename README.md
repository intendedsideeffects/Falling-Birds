# Falling Birds

*Falling Birds* is an interactive data story about **biodiversity loss**, blending data analysis, sound and storytelling. It is the first part of **LOSS**, a two-part series exploring disappearance in the natural world.

The experience follows a flock of birds across a timeline, each representing a real species. As extinction events unfold, birds begin to fall from the sky. Viewers can explore individual species, hear their calls, read their stories and contribute stories of their own.

Rather than presenting biodiversity loss through numbers alone, the project uses data, sound and narrative to create space for reflection, grief and connection.

**Built with:** Blender · Python · React
